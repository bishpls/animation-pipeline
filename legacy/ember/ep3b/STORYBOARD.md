# EMBER III · 点火 KINDLE, hand-animated

No video models are used. Every frame is drawn by code (`src/`) as a pure function of *t*. Image generation was used only for three style reference frames (`refs/`). **64.0 s** at 150 BPM (beat = 0.4 s), 160 beats, 24 fps, animated on twos with ones for smears.

**Style:** Promare-flat. Hard single-tone cel shadows, purple-black tapered line, geometric triangle fire in pink, orange and yellow, flat layered pines, a halftone moon. The void is pure black with cold cyan rims and eyes.
