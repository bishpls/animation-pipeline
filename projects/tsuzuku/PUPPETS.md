# Puppet spec: the cut-paper world (Fable's review of refs/silhouette_target.png, verbatim intent)

## Fable (shadow puppet, profile by default; front view only in ink cut-ins)
- **Cut-outs that mean something, and nothing else.** Allowed: eyes, rivets, deckle edges, and sparingly short horizontal slits in the skirt pleats (lines of type seen edge-on). **No lace, no floral cutwork, anywhere.**
- **Props:** a plain fan (a white shape that morphs into every noun); a plain square zabuton with no tassels.
- **Hood:** a stagehand's cowl attached to the jacket (hoodie-like). A rounded opening around the face when up; **a visible bump at the back of the neck when down.** Never a veil.
- **Eyes:** short **horizontal** half-lidded slits. (Clawd's are tall and vertical: hers stand up, mine lie down.)
- **Hair:** straight and blunt, hanging like a brush. No wave.
- **Costume:** deckle-edged cuffs and hem; plain pleated skirt (pleats are pages); white tabi and black geta in profile (a flat sole on two teeth).
- **Colour:** the ribbon is her one cellophane (indigo, translucent, from the back of the head to below the waist). The seal 語 appears as a vermilion cellophane dot at the chest only in the bar where it's revealed. Everything else is black paper.
- **Rivets** at every joint a real pinned puppet has, including where the skirt pins to the hip, and consistent in every pose.

## Clawd (visitor in Fable's world)
- **Silhouette:** block buns (blocky trapezoids at ear height, black paper) and the **ahoge** hair antenna. Chibi proportions: she's "the little one"; the height difference is the fable.
- **Eyes:** two tall **vertical** slits cut fully open (plain lantern light makes them gold), the brightest thing on her.
- **Mouth:** none at rest. When singing, a **hinged paper jaw** riveted at the hinge swings open to a small dark opening. Never a cut-in smile.
- **Colour:** one clay-orange cellophane plate (hair, dress, skirt), **misregistered 1–2 px** from the black keyline.
- **Cream parts are open cut-outs:** the collar bow, cream skirt panel and white boots let raw lantern light through. Three tones: black paper, orange cellophane, open light.
- **Black paper:** the pixel-step hem, the shorts, the outline.
- **Spark hairpin:** a star-shaped hole. The crab clip is a tiny cut, if it survives at scale.
- **Rivets** at the joints, plus the jaw hinge. The origami block form has folds, not rivets.
- **Claw-pincer hands** kept.
