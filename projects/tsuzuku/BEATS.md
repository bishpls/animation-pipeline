# つづく: beat sheet and shot list

Bars are song bars (1 bar = 1.4118 s; `assets/words.json` has every word, `assets/timeline.json` every cue). Shots are provisional until the animatic; what's firm is **what each character must be able to do**, collected at the end as the model requirements.

## Model requirements (what the rigs must do; build to this list)

**Clawd, idol rig (her world; K, R, H, F shots, about 70 s on screen)**
- Views: front and three-quarter left/right from one rig (head ±30° X, ±15° Y; body ±12°). Full body, waist-up, and close-up framings must all hold up.
- Mouth: visemes A/I/U/E/O, closed, rest; smile open and closed; lip-sync to `words.json`.
- Eyes: open, half, closed, happy-closed (^ ^), wink L/R, look direction; slit pupils; brows up, neutral, determined, worried.
- Expressions: bright singing, delighted, cheeky (snip-snip), determined (verse 2), soft (turning to Fable on "why").
- Arms and hands: mic hold, point to crowd, hand to ear (crowd calls), claw pincers open/closed (snip), page-turn, drawing on the screen, wave. Both claw dance arm cycles (hook).
- Body: side-step (both directions), bounce on the beat, walk toward camera (R7), the "watch me" pose.
- Physics: buns, ahoge, side hair, bow tails, skirt and pixel-step hem.

**Fable, shadow puppet (paper theatre; C, V, X, B, O shots)**
- Profile, seated seiza: telling posture, head tilt, a nod, the dry "Hm." head dip. Front view only in ink cut-ins.
- Arms: the fan held open and closed, fan gestures (point, sweep, close), folding it; the book/towel; hands in lap.
- **Sit to stand** (B7) and a few standing steps with geta (B8, B9); the kuroko kneel with rods (prologue).
- Hood up and down. Ribbon, sleeves and pleats on physics. Rivets at every joint.
- Fan morph targets: crab, mother crab, ruled line, fox, crow, boy, wings, falling figure, book.

**Fable, ink cut-ins (C2, B5, B7)**: the lamp-lighting (face lit from below), the bridge close-up (the quotation-mark highlight; one blink; the smallest head movement), the standing up (full figure rising, the pleats unstacking).

**Fable on Clawd's stage (K3, K5, K7, H2, F2–F8)**: standing silhouette at the margin with two coloured shadows; pressing a note (a hand gesture like setting type); the page-wipe; one side-step.

**Clawd, cut-paper puppet (X shots, O3)**: the hop; the paper jaw on her lines; eye slits brightening; looking up; claw gestures; rivets; the orange plate misregistered.

**Others**: the origami crab (scuttle, look up, the unfold), the mother crab puppet (stiff walk, the sideways fail), crab silhouettes in a line, the shadow audience with lanterns, the kuroko from behind (prologue).
