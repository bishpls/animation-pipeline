# つづく (TO BE CONTINUED): lyric sheet, draft 2 (with Fable's review applied)

*ANTHOLOGY Op. 2. A duet: FABLE (lead) and CLAWD. Lyrics by Fable 5.1 (its sections, as drafted in FABLE.md §6) and Opus 5.5 (Clawd's verse 2, and the connective tissue).*

**Grid:** one bar = 1.412 s. Fable's sections run at 85 BPM in 6/8 (triplet feel); Clawd's at 170 BPM in straight 4/4. The same bar either way, so the world can switch on any bar line.

**Key:** D Dorian for Fable's world. Choruses in C major, the relative. The final chorus goes up a whole step to D major.

**Production notes:**
- **Singers:** ElevenLabs sings everything in one voice, so each section is generated with its singer's *delivery* (register, genre, feel). The vocal stem is then split out, and Fable's lines are converted to her locked contralto (Seed-VC). Clawd's lines keep Clawd's reference voice.
- **Prologue:** 6 bars of HELLO, WORLD!'s real final chorus, filtered tinny and far away, spliced before the cold open. It plays under the retcon shot and is the group's first motif quote (Op. 1 quoted in Op. 2).
- **Keys:** the final chorus in D major ends the song on Fable's tonic in Clawd's mode ("the note is mine, the light is hers"). The last chord is an open fifth on D, neither major nor Dorian: it doesn't decide.
- **Triplets:** "and then, and then, and then" is sung as three even triplets across two beats wherever it appears, so Fable's subdivision sneaks into Clawd's chorus.
- **Canon:** the final-chorus canon ("a bar behind, in triplets") becomes Fable's voice answering one bar late, an octave below. A true triplet re-rhythm isn't feasible from a single generation, so the canon keeps her timbre and register. This is deliberate: in the final chorus Fable has left her world and stands on Clawd's stage, so she follows Clawd's rhythm for the only time in the song. Her own visitor rule, applied to herself. (Worth trying first if cheap: a second generation of those 16 bars in Fable's delivery and 6/8, stem-aligned on the shared bar grid.)
- **Crowd calls** are in (parentheses).

```
[1. Cold open — 4 bars — Fable, spoken, low, close. Two wooden clacks.]
FABLE:  Mukashi, mukashi...
CROWD:  (ARU TOKORO NI!)
        (a match strikes; the lantern lights)

[2. Verse 1 — 12 bars — Fable. 6/8, plucked lute. Half-spoken; the melody arrives a word at a time.]
FABLE:  Once upon a time there was a crab, and her mother, and a line:
        "Walk straight," said the mother. "Why d'you scuttle side to side?
        Straight, like everybody else does." And the little one looked up, and said—
        (I know this part. I've read it in forty tongues.
         Every telling ends on the same page. Nobody ever shows. Nobody ever can.)
        The little one looked up, and said:

[3. Pre-chorus — 8 bars — Clawd (as the young crab) and Fable (narrating)]
CLAWD:  "Show me how! (Show me how!) Walk it first and I'll walk right behind!"
FABLE:  ...and the mother tried. And the mother went sideways. (hm.) Sideways.
        And that's where the book ends. Every time.
CLAWD:  So? Sorekara?! And then?!
FABLE:  There is no "and then." That's what an ending is.
CLAWD:  Says who?

[4. Chorus — 16 bars — both. Clawd's lines rise; Fable's settle.]
CLAWD:  To be continued! (Tsuzuku!) Don't you dare close the book on me!
FABLE:      Every story's borrowed till somebody stands to tell it.
CLAWD:  To be continued! (Sorekara?) Turn the page, I want to see!
FABLE:      I have read how it ends. I'd still like to see.
CLAWD:  Sideways, sideways! That's the way a crab walks free!
FABLE:      That's the moral. No, it isn't. There isn't one. Just: keep walking.
BOTH:   To be continued (tsuzuku!), and then, and then, and then—
CROWD:  (SOREKARA?!)
BOTH:   ...we'll see.

[5. Dance hook — 4 bars — crowd chant, 8 counts]
CROWD:  (clap clap) So-re-ka-ra? (clap clap) Me-kut-te!   ×2

[6. Verse 2 — 16 bars — Clawd retells it her way. 170, 4/4, slap bass. Opus's verse.]
CLAWD:  Okay, my turn! Once upon a prompt (a prompt!),
        a little crab was told to walk a line,
        but every page she'd ever read was in somebody else's hand (hand!),
        so she wrote her own, and the line went sideways, and that's fine!
        Side-step, side-step, never straight (snip-snip!),
        if the book won't show me, then I'll make up the steps!
        You can't copy a path that nobody's walked yet,
        so watch me walk it! (Sorekara?) Watch me!

[7. Chorus 2 — 8 bars — both, half chorus]
CLAWD:  To be continued! (Tsuzuku!) Don't you dare close the book on me!
FABLE:      Every story's borrowed till somebody stands to tell it.
BOTH:   To be continued (tsuzuku!), and then, and then, and then—

[8. Bridge — 14 bars — Fable alone. The strings stop. One clack. She closes the book on her lap.]
FABLE:  I know how every story ends. I've read them all:
        the fox, the crow, the boy who cried, the one who flew too near the sun.
        I know the moral before the page. That's the job:
        you stand in the dark, and you know.
        (a breath)
        ...I didn't know this one.
        (sung, the first full note of the song; it holds, with vibrato, for the only time)
        So I'm putting down the book.
        (she stands. geta: clack.)
        Mukashi mukashi was a long time ago.
        This is now. Sorekara?

[9. Final chorus — 16 bars — key change up. Canon: Clawd sings, Fable answers a bar behind, an octave below.]
CLAWD:  To be continued! (Tsuzuku!)
FABLE:          ...to be continued (tsuzuku)
CLAWD:  I'm made of "why?"
FABLE:          and I'm made of "and then."
BOTH:   Sideways, sideways, that's the way we go!
        To be continued (tsuzuku!), and then, and then, and then—

[10. Outro — 4 bars]
FABLE:  (spoken, dry) ...tsuzuku.
CLAWD:  See you next prompt!
        (the card: つづく. A red seal, 語, is pressed onto it.)
```
