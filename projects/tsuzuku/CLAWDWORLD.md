# Clawd's world: choreography and spectacle (Fable's rulings, on her feature song)

Michael asked for Fable's view on Clawd's sections, since つづく is Fable's feature song. These are her decisions (September 2026 review of `src/chorus.js`), and they govern Clawd's world A (bars 45–93) and the final chorus. Where she marks something as hers (the paper, the cards, the notes), the paper-world session builds it; the seams are in `HANDOFF.md`.

## The frame
- Clawd's stage sits inside a **thin washi border (3–4% of frame) in every shot**. The camera moves the picture inside the frame, never the frame (Fable's butai rule, in Clawd's world).
- **LEDs show Clawd's words; paper shows Fable's.** Nothing of Fable's ever appears on an LED.

## The hook (bars 62–66): the fan-copyable dance
- Hand to ear on "So-re-ka-ra?" (the question comes in); two snips on the crowd's claps ("a crab's clap is a snip; the hall claps, she snips"); the page-wipe on "Me-kut-te!". The wipe is the one move everyone shares.
- **Both wipes go the same way, left to right** (a Japanese page lifts from the left). Alternating sides would un-turn the page and give fans two moves.
- **The wipe's edge on the screens tracks her hand's x: her hand is the transition.**
  - Wipe 1 (bar 63): only the screens change. The chorus light show wipes to a page of Fable's book (verse 1's print: crab, mother, ruled line on washi) and takes chorus 1's margin notes with it.
  - Wipe 2 (bar 65): bigger. Screens, LED floor and lights turn together to the next page, and **Fable makes the wipe with her, on the same beat, from the front row** (a flat hand across the bottom of the frame): you can't tell whose wipe did it. The hall wipes with her both times.
- **One locked full-body shot for the four bars, no cuts.** Fans learn it from one take, and it's the vertical cut.

## Verse 2 (bars 66–82): the screens are kamishibai cards, and Fable pulls them
- 67–72: the pictures behind Clawd are Fable's (her cut-paper crab and line; at 70, the pages in other scripts), and **Fable turns them**: she turns the page of the book in her lap, and the screen's card slides in on the same drawing. Two over-the-shoulder cuts show the cause: 67–68 (her chorus-1 notes turn over to the crab and the line, landing on "prompt") and 70–70.7 (the Esopo page). The page shows what the card shows. **No hand of hers ever appears on Clawd's stage or screens** (Michael found the cut-paper hand at the screen's edge awkward; Fable: "a small black hand at a screen edge is a stray cutout, whatever medium it's rendered in").
- 72, "she wrote her own": Clawd's claw draws a pixel crab and a pixel line on Fable's washi page. A pixel line going sideways is a staircase: her hem motif. Fable's hands come to rest.
- From then Fable is still, hands empty, watching. So Clawd's wipe at 76 brings a **blank page**: nobody is pulling.
- "Make up the steps": pixel footprints appear where she stamps, and the readers copy her.
- 77.75–80: the path draws itself on the floor under her feet. **No straight walk to camera**: the lyric says never straight. Diagonal side-steps with a push-in.

## Fable in the hall (her ruling, after Michael found her at the wing awkward)
- **Front row, house-left, seated seiza on her cushion, seen from behind.** Hood up, the book open on her lap, her lantern lit on the floor at her left: the one lantern in the hall never raised. (First ruled "held low at the knee"; both Fable consults then moved it to the floor at her left, where the paper world keeps it after Michael's note on the lap lantern, "so the cut is lamp to lamp".) Her hands rest on her thighs when idle (seiza hands). Geta paired beside the cushion. Her face is never shown in world A; the bridge close-up keeps its reveal. She's the nearest reader to the camera, a foreground plane in the bottom-left of the wides that leaves the frame in every push-in.
- **Illustrated, full colour, from FABLE_canon** (FABLE.md §7: "the puppet survives the illustration"): brass rivets at the joints catch the light; she takes Clawd's light (pink rim on one edge, cyan on the other, like every reader); no cast shadows in A. - **Motion on twos with holds, in a world on ones.** A nod on each downbeat (her pulse is Clawd's half-note); the ribbon alone sways on the offbeat, her dance delegated to the bookmark. One seated weight-shift each way on "Sideways, sideways". Hook: hand to ear on "Sorekara?", two low claps with the hall, still for wipe 1, the flat-hand wipe with Clawd on wipe 2. - **The last bar of world A** (91.5–93) pushes in on her as the hall goes dark: her lantern is the last light, and the cut on the hyoshigi is a match to B1, seated on the same cushion with the same lantern (the paper world's lamp). ## Margin notes on Clawd's stage
- Pressed in the **bottom margin** of the washi border, like a caption under an illustration: Caslon italic, the lighter ink, one line, on the crowd call while Clawd has her hand to her ear. They're what Fable is writing in the book in her lap (she writes each one; the over-the-shoulder cut at 67 shows them on the page she turns).
- They accumulate; the first wipe clears them.
- **Clawd never looks at them** until "why" in F3.

## Choreography principles (the duet's argument, visible)
- **Clawd never lands.** Each phrase ends rising: toes, chin, a hand still travelling. The settle belongs to the crowd's call, not her word. **The crabs land the downbeats for her.**
- Springs are Clawd's. Fable gets no follow springs: twos, snap, hold, one gesture per note.
- One exception, the fable's own: **the sideways step (bars 54, 74.25, 135) lands on the downbeat, weight low, feet flat.** It's the move she borrowed, and the one they share in F5.

## Vetoes
