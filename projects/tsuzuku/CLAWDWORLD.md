# Clawd's world: choreography and spectacle (Fable's rulings, on her feature song)

Michael asked for Fable's view on Clawd's sections, since つづく is Fable's feature song. These are her decisions (September 2026 review of `src/chorus.js`), and they govern Clawd's world A (bars 45–93) and the final chorus. Where she marks something as hers (the paper, the cards, the notes), the paper-world session builds it; the seams are in `HANDOFF.md`.

## The frame
- Clawd's stage sits inside a **thin washi border (3–4% of frame) in every shot**. The camera moves the picture inside the frame, never the frame (Fable's butai rule, in Clawd's world).
- **LEDs show Clawd's words; paper shows Fable's.** Nothing of Fable's ever appears on an LED.

## The hook (bars 62–66): the fan-copyable dance
- Hand to ear on "So-re-ka-ra?" (the question comes in); two snips on the crowd's claps ("a crab's clap is a snip; the hall claps, she snips"); the page-wipe on "Me-kut-te!". The wipe is the one move everyone shares.
- **Both wipes go the same way, left to right** (a Japanese page lifts from the left). Alternating sides would un-turn the page and give fans two moves.
- **The wipe's edge on the screens tracks her hand's x: her hand is the transition.**
  - Wipe 1 (bar 63): only the screens change. The chorus light show wipes to a page of Fable's book (verse 1's print: crab, mother, ruled line on washi) and takes chorus 1's margin notes with it.
  - Wipe 2 (bar 65): bigger. Screens, LED floor and lights turn together to the next page, and **Fable makes the wipe with her, on the same beat, from the front row** (a flat hand across the bottom of the frame): you can't tell whose wipe did it. The hall wipes with her both times.
- **One locked full-body shot for the four bars, no cuts.** Fans learn it from one take, and it's the vertical cut.

## Verse 2 (bars 66–82): the screens are kamishibai cards, and Fable pulls them
- 67–72: the pictures behind Clawd are Fable's (her cut-paper crab and line; at 70, the pages in other scripts), and **Fable turns them**: she turns the page of the book in her lap, and the screen's card slides in on the same drawing. Two over-the-shoulder cuts show the cause: 67–68 (her chorus-1 notes turn over to the crab and the line, landing on "prompt") and 70–70.7 (the Esopo page). The page shows what the card shows. **No hand of hers ever appears on Clawd's stage or screens** (Michael found the cut-paper hand at the screen's edge awkward; Fable: "a small black hand at a screen edge is a stray cutout, whatever medium it's rendered in").
- 72, "she wrote her own": Clawd's claw draws a pixel crab and a pixel line on Fable's washi page. A pixel line going sideways is a staircase: her hem motif. Fable's hands come to rest.
- From then Fable is still, hands empty, watching. So Clawd's wipe at 76 brings a **blank page**: nobody is pulling.
- "Make up the steps": pixel footprints appear where she stamps, and the readers copy her.
- 77.75–80: the path draws itself on the floor under her feet. **No straight walk to camera**: the lyric says never straight. Diagonal side-steps with a push-in.

## World A is a card in Fable's butai (Michael's structure; Fable's ruling, 2026-09-25)
Michael felt the seated hall Fable was "pasted over" the video and asked for Clawd's world to play inside the box that holds the bridge, with a meta layer beyond the stage that Fable occupies. Fable: "Her world is a card in my theatre; I sit outside it."
- **The frame.** Close views are the card itself; the washi deckle border is the card's edge. Pulled back, the card sits in the butai window's rail, with the doors, the room, the readers, and Fable. The rail never shows in close views. (Room shots film the card with the paper world's `stage()`; see `src/idolstage.js` roomFrame.)
- **Fable in her room.** Seated seiza at the butai's right (the teller's side, where B9 stands her: "I stand where I sat"), three-quarters from behind, facing the window. The illustrated rig (`rig/fable_seated`, drawn mirrored to face the window), lit dark: her lantern the bridge's warm chōchin, on the floor at her left between her and the butai, the room's only warm light; Clawd's pink and cyan on her hood's edge. All lanterns, hall and room, are oblong like hers; hers alone is warm (Michael: "it makes Fable stand out from the crowd").
- **One audience.** The hall inside the card and the room outside it use the same readers (the paper world's silhouettes and lantern; Michael preferred them). In the hall they're rim-lit pink and cyan by Clawd's stage. In the room they keep clear of Fable (`audience`'s gap).
- **Beats (bars).** 43.2 (from the tear): the window at the telling camera. 45: one bar wide, the room flooded with her light; push in, full frame by 47. 48: over Fable's shoulder, half a bar, writing the note. 52, 56: one bar each, writing, then a small head bob side to side. Hook: no cuts. 65: wipe 2 is a card pull seen from inside, its paper edge crossing the whole picture with Clawd's hand, the next card behind it; she stays because she's live. 67, 70: hiki-nuki from the room (one bar, half a bar): Fable reaches up and pulls the card, and the screens change as its paper edge passes. 72: hands to thighs, still. 82, 88: one bar each, clapping with the hall, bobbing, the seated sideways shift. 91.5: Clawd's curtsy as the lights die, pulling back into the room. 93: black on the clack; the lantern alone glides to B1's position, and B1 fades in around it (built on the paper side).
- **Annotating, watching, joining: the escalation is the story.** Michael's arc: a little head bob side to side, then clapping along, really enjoying herself.
- **76: not blank.** Nobody is pulling, so the page carries only verse 1's ruled line, "walk straight". Her pixel footprints stamp across it on "make up the steps"; the line breaks into her staircase on "watch me walk it"; 「つづく→」 lands in pixel in the bottom-right corner, the arrow pointing to the teller's side, where cards go.
- **F (final chorus; Fable's ruling, after Michael asked to finish it).**
  - **The fold (177.8–181): no curtain; the push-in is the fold.** The wide opens with her standing at the butai's right, lantern in hand (B9's end). The window blazes as Clawd's stage powers up, and under that flood the paper silhouette becomes the illustrated rig, same position, rivets flaring first. At 178–179.2 she sets the lantern on her cushion beside the closed book: "the narrator's tools stay on the seat; what steps in has empty hands". At 179.2–181 the camera pushes through the window, the rail leaves frame, and she passes out of frame right. At 181, on the downbeat, the stage is full-frame; she steps in from the right and pushes the hood back on the second step.
  - **On her stage:** hood back, facing the hall, one step upstage at Clawd's stage-left (house right), two coloured shadows on the floor. Canon for F2–F4: Clawd's pose a bar late, half amplitude, on twos. From the sideways step at 135, unison (still half amplitude, still on twos): "the retelling catches the telling and stays caught". They freeze together at 141. Clawd turns on "why" and finds a face.
  - **The room** is gone from 181 and returns once, inside the freeze: the hit at 141 in close; at about 142, the wide: the frozen picture in the window, the light ringing out onto the empty cushion, lantern lit, book closed. It's the last image before the outro.
  - **Notes:** F4 presses her line in the card's bottom margin, full ink, as she sings it, no hand. F6 is a BOTH line: on Clawd's LEDs and in her margin at once, the same words in two media. F8: the margin empty, the page before つづく.
  - What F does that A didn't: her face; the rail out of frame; full ink, then none; the delay closing.
- **The MV layer** (MOTION.md §9, inside the card only, never on Fable's paper): beat-punch zooms on the chorus downbeats, the drop and "Ikuzo!" (none in the hook); three-colour afterimages on the page-wipes, "Ikuzo!" and "watch me walk it"; slam-in sticker type for the crowd calls on the side screens; glitch strips at the power-up and the tape-stop; a colour fringe on the drop, "Ikuzo!" and the turning edges.
- **No zoom-cuts to Fable in the choruses** (Michael: disruptive). She's in frame at the window's side throughout; the cuts to her are the over-the-shoulder ones (48, 67, 70, 80.2), which carry the book.
- **Retired:** the hall Fable, the lap page turns and over-the-shoulder page cuts, and the illustrated back-view crowd.

## Margin notes on Clawd's stage
- Pressed in the **bottom margin** of the washi border, like a caption under an illustration: Caslon italic, the lighter ink, one line, on the crowd call while Clawd has her hand to her ear. They're what Fable is writing in the book in her lap (she writes each one; the over-the-shoulder cut at 48 shows them on her page).
- They accumulate; the first wipe clears them.
- **Clawd never looks at them** until "why" in F3.

## Choreography principles (the duet's argument, visible)
- **Clawd never lands.** Each phrase ends rising: toes, chin, a hand still travelling. The settle belongs to the crowd's call, not her word. **The crabs land the downbeats for her.**
- Springs are Clawd's. Fable gets no follow springs: twos, snap, hold, one gesture per note.
- One exception, the fable's own: **the sideways step (bars 54, 74.25, 135) lands on the downbeat, weight low, feet flat.** It's the move she borrowed, and the one they share in F5.
