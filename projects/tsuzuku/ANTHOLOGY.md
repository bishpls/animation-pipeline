## The governing reference: Symphogear

For the human collaborator, the project is heavily informed by *Senki Zesshō Symphogear*, specifically:
- **Every character's song is her worldview made audible.** A member's genre, tempo, instruments and vocal delivery express who she is, not a style chosen for variety.
- **Composite songs keep each voice's DNA intact.** In duets and unisons, each singer keeps her own melodic identity (contour, rhythm feel, register), and the song is the negotiation between those identities. They don't dissolve into one blended voice.
- **Motifs interweave and accumulate.** Each member's leitmotif is learned in her own song, then quoted, answered and combined in later songs. A final four-member unison should braid all four theme cards together.

## Rules for every song

- **Leitmotifs are musical, visual and technical:** each member owns a sound, a look and a rendering technique. When the song focuses on a member, the whole video enters her world.
- **Visitors keep their identity; the host world changes their medium.** (Fable's rule: in her world, Clawd becomes riveted cut paper with a cellophane colour plate.)
- **A shared grid:** 170 BPM straight 4/4 = 85 BPM 6/8, one bar = 1,412 ms, so worlds can switch on any bar line. Future members should choose tempos and feels that divide or multiply this bar.
- **Each member designs herself** through a subagent of her model, with full authority over her identity. Opus produces and executes, and the member reviews anything touching her identity. Her design doc is saved verbatim and wins conflicts.
- **Contrast in one sentence** (Fable on Clawd): "Her lines go up like a question mark. Mine come down like a full stop." Every new member should find her own sentence.
- **Opus numbering:** the singles are numbered Op. 1, Op. 2, …

## Voices (locked with singing-voice conversion, one reference per member)

- **Clawd:** high mezzo/soprano, bright and forward, a smile in the tone; upward leaps on hook syllables; phrases end high; always addressing a crowd; the shy spoken aside as her tell. Comfortable A3–E5, belting to F#5. Idol Japanese formulas.
- **Fable:** contralto/low mezzo (F3–C5); dry, warm, close, straight tone; speech becomes song; vibrato on one held note per song only. Folktale formulas (mukashi mukashi, sorekara, tsuzuku).
