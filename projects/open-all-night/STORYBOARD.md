# OPEN ALL NIGHT *(a love song for the ads)*: storyboard

**Logline.** At 2 a.m. the neon OPEN sign of an all-night bakery flickers on. Upstairs, a kid who can't sleep has the whole world open to her. Beneath the city, an invisible machine pays for it in the blink of an eye. By sunrise the baker's one little ad has fed the whole town, and everyone sings a love song to the machinery nobody thanks.

**One building, one night.** Everything happens in or around one apartment block. The **bakery** is at street level, with the OPEN sign in its window (bakers bake all night). **The kid** lives upstairs, and **Dad** fixes the sink in their kitchen. **Grandma** is in Rome, where it's morning. The film runs from 2 a.m. to sunrise.

**Medium.** A mid-century cut-paper title sequence (Saul Bass, *Catch Me If You Can*) printed on a four-ink risograph, with Swiss kinetic type. Every frame should hold up as a poster.
- **Inks:** blue `#3148d0` (night, the machine), fluorescent pink `#ff4d9d` (neon, joy), yellow `#ffcc22` (lamplight, windows, dawn) and black `#1d1a22` (silhouettes, type), on warm stock `#f1eadb`. Overprints give violet, vermilion, olive and near-black for free.
- **Light is paper.** Lit things are knockouts (paper showing through ink), and glow is a halftoned halo. No digital gradients or blur-glows.
- **Large fields are solid ink.** Tints (halftone) are only for glows, shading and texture accents. A tinted sky reads as noise.
- **Cut-paper figures.** Characters are silhouettes made of a few cut shapes with hand-cut edges that boil on twos. Faces are minimal: a dot or eye cut-out at most, never Corporate-Memphis limbs.
- **Motion.** Characters on twos, camera and type on ones. Every hit lands on a beat or a sung syllable, and every shot has an event.

**Type system**
- **Verse lyrics:** Archivo, wdth 72, wght 800, about 64 px. Words print on at their sung time (a quick rise with overshoot) and the line clears when it ends. The slot is chosen per shot (see the table) and set in knockout on floods, black on paper. **Key words** switch to Instrument Serif Italic in an accent ink.
- **Hook lyrics (choruses):** the type is the image. Archivo at wdth 62 / wght 900, full frame, slammed word by word.
- **Ad-libs** (`(all night!)`, `(a glance!)`, `(blink!)`, `(here's one!)`) are the choir. They're set as rubber stamps: smaller, rotated and in a second ink.

**Colour script.** Night blue (intro, V1) → black and pink (the machine, the blink) → pink explosion (chorus 1) → dawn yellow (V2, bakery) → newsprint black and yellow (history) → deep blue (bridge, the gears) → sunrise, every ink at once (final chorus) → soft morning with the sign still glowing (outro).

**Motifs.** The **OPEN sign** (access). The **eye** (attention: "a glance", "the blink"). **Windows** (lit apartments = browser windows = people). The **coin/penny** (someone pays). The **paper plane** (an ad in flight).

**Beat grid.** 124 BPM, first downbeat 0.106 s, bar = 1.935 s. Bar *n* starts at `0.106 + 1.9355 n`. Times below are seconds.

## Checks
- **Hook in the first second:** the sign starts flickering at 0.14 with the a cappella hook.
- **Every shot has an event,** and every chorus line has one idea.
- **Rhymes:** the sign opens and closes the film; the eye appears in the pre-chorus and in both choruses, blinking, glancing and then winking; the globe becomes the O.
- **Text in the scene beyond the lyrics:** none, except the OPEN sign itself and the title.
- **Lyric safe areas:** each shot's composition leaves its lyric slot clear.
