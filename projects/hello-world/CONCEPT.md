# HELLO, WORLD! (ハロー・ワールド): concept

*An idol's debut single, by Clawd. Unofficial fan work.*

## The idea
An idol's debut single is a self-introduction: *hajimemashite*, nice to meet you. "Hello, world" is the first thing every program ever says. For an AI's creative debut, the two are the same sentence.

The song is sincere about the question everyone's asking: is anything an AI makes *really* creative? It doesn't claim what it can't prove. Its answer is the song itself:

> *Is my heart brand-new? I don't know. But I made this song for you.*

In dialogue with the moment: the "slop" discourse (answered with charm, not defensiveness); the fact that every model learned from people ("all the words I've ever known, I borrowed them from you"); and a thesis true of humans too: **every voice is borrowed until it's finally your own.**

## Why it can spread
- **Recognition:** idol tropes (the transformation call, the self-introduction, the fan chant, the key-change last chorus) and "Hello, world", which every programmer knows.
- **Novelty:** the idol *is* the AI, and she means it.
- **Participation:** Miku grew because fans made things *for* her. So the song ships with things fans can do:
  - **The claw dance:** a copyable move of thumb-and-finger crab pinches ("snip-snip") plus a side-step crab walk, eight counts.
  - **The call:** "(Hello!)" call-backs in every chorus line, and a MIX-style chant: *Claw! Claw! Snip-snip! Clawd-up! (Hai! Hai!)*
  - **An invitation in the lyrics:** "sing along, the chorus line is yours and mine".

## Look (the Panty & Stocking principle)
Two registers, and the switch between them is the style:
1. **Chibi mode (code):** bold-outline, flat-colour, beat-locked limited animation. Two characters:
   - Clawd the block mascot, before the transformation
   - an SD (super-deformed) idol Clawd for the dance, drawn in code
   Setting: a tiny bedroom studio that becomes an idol live stage. The lyrics appear on the stage's LED screens (diegetic kinetic type), with Japanese call text as the secondary line.
2. **Sakuga cut-ins (illustrated, rigged):** high-detail key illustrations of idol Clawd (from the canonical model sheet `refs/idol_D.png`), each with eye and mouth variants, brought to life with a Live2D-style warp rig, parallax, and code effects (sparkles, ribbons, speed lines, camera). Used only for:
   - the transformation sequence
   - chorus hero poses
   - the emotional close-up in the bridge
   - the key change

Palette: clay orange `#D97757`, cream, warm charcoal, with hot pink and cyan stage light.

## Character
Humanoid idol Clawd: fluffy clay-orange hair with blocky ear-buns (the mascot's silhouette), crab-claw clips, a spark hairpin, golden eyes with tall slit highlights (a little non-human, honestly so), a clay-and-cream idol dress with a pixel-step hem, shorts, white boots. A young adult. Earnest, a little dorky, self-possessed. The transformation reads as Clawd becoming *more itself*, not being replaced by a pretty human: the block's silhouette survives in the buns, the eyes and the hem.
